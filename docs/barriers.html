Fitting is not reaching

Two extra nodes.
Three rewrite steps.

The source x*(1+1) needs five nodes. The target x+x needs only three. Both fit at five, but the full five-node closure excludes the target. A seven-node intermediate unlocks it.

The weighted constant product is the counter-control: its cheaper addition-only candidates still cannot be reached through seven nodes. A finite cap is not an impossibility result for larger budgets.

Why the lower obstruction is complete

For every fitting lower level, independent parent-edge and closure checking proves the entire reachable set. Exclusion is therefore a bounded impossibility certificate, not a timeout. The first successful level supplies a shortest path within that level. The same checker independently enumerates every polynomial-equivalent candidate in the full cap grammar.

A browser report is independently checked only after rewritelab verify-atlas evidence.json accepts it. Every nested report retains the complete base evidence.

The exact boundary

Least allowed budget.
Not the smallest possible universe.

The allowed budgets are 3, 5 and 7 nodes. First fitting budget counts both source and target. Extra budget is first reachable minus first fitting; it is null when no proof is reached through the cap. For a leaf-only proof, first allowed budget is still 3, even if the path uses just one node. A shortest path at that level need not minimize steps over larger budgets, nor have a peak equal to that allowed budget.

The grammar, costs and bidirectional rules are exactly those of the base lab: ordered trees over 0, 1, x, y, + and *, positive integral per-occurrence costs 1..8, full reverse-zero introduction, and a hard seven-node cap. Semantic identity holds over rational arithmetic, established by a degree-bounded exact 5×5 grid argument, not empirical sampling. Source-too-large, target-too-large, blocked, first-reachable and already-reachable are distinct states.

This explicit finite rewrite graph is not an e-graph, learned policy, tensor engine, floating-point compiler proof, new algorithm or speed benchmark. No claim about reachability beyond the chosen cap. Barriers are properties of this specified grammar and rules, not universal compiler lower bounds. See BARRIERS.md, CONTRACT.md and SOURCES.md.
